# Fire Detection & Industrial Risk Assessment

### Process Safety Engineering | I2E Internship – 2026

## Project Overview
This internship focused on the assessment of fire detection systems and the modelling of industrial accident scenarios involving diesel storage tanks and other industrial installations.

The objective was to evaluate detection coverage, identify potential hazards, and support the improvement of industrial safety measures through numerical simulation.

## Engineering Tools
- **Detect3D** – Fire detector coverage assessment and optimization.
- **PHAST** – Modelling of industrial accident consequences and hazardous scenarios.

## Methodology
- Modelled fire detection coverage for two diesel storage tanks, considering rim seal fire scenarios.
- Evaluated detector arrangements using 1ooN and 2ooN voting configurations.
- Analysed industrial accident scenarios and their potential consequences using PHAST.
- Interpreted simulation results to assess detection performance and industrial risks.

## Key Results
| Indicator | Result |
|---|---|
| Diesel storage tanks studied | 2 |
| Fire detectors | 4 per tank |
| Detection coverage – 1ooN | 94.3% |
| Detection coverage – 2ooN | 78.9% |
| Detection model resolution | 1.25 m |

*Results correspond to the modelled configurations and assumptions.*
